## Wording for the psychologist to check

These passages contain no unknown facts, but they describe how the practice works in general terms. The psychologist should confirm that each matches their practice before publishing:

- **Home, "How it works"; Services, "What to expect"; FAQ, first appointment and number of sessions:** what a first appointment involves, agreeing and reviewing a plan together, and being free to stop at any point.
- **Services, "Confidentiality"; FAQ, "Is what I say confidential?":** the limits of confidentiality, and that sharing would be discussed first wherever it is possible and safe.
- **Privacy policy:** the kinds of information collected from clients, the lawful bases and Article 9 conditions in the table, who information is shared with, and that no decisions are made by automated processing alone. The data controller should check the whole policy against how the practice actually handles records.
- **Contact, "When you write":** the request to keep first emails short and free of detailed personal or medical information.
