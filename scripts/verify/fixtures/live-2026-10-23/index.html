How to book all five

Booking a free session

  • Choose a day and time that suits you in the online booking calendar.
  • You only need your name and email address to book.
  • Book each session separately, one at a time.
  • Your confirmation email has links to move or cancel the session.

Getting started

Booking your five free sessions

  1. Book your first session

    Choose a day and time in the booking calendar, then confirm it with your name and email address. Calendly emails you a confirmation.

  2. Book the next one

    Calendly books one session at a time. Once it has confirmed a session, choose “Book another session” under the calendar on the booking page, or open the booking page again, and pick your next time.

  3. Repeat until all five are booked

    Each session has its own confirmation email, with links to move or cancel it.

Need help now?

DeepRest is not an emergency service and cannot respond to urgent requests. If you need help now, please don’t wait for a reply from us.

  • 999

    Emergency. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, or a life is at risk, call 999 or go to your nearest A&E.

  • NHS 111

    Urgent, but not an emergency. Call 111 for urgent medical help or advice.

  • 116 123

    Samaritans. Free to call, day or night, 365 days a year, whatever you’re going through.
