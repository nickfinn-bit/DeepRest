Calendly, our booking system

Sessions are booked through Calendly, provided by Calendly, LLC in the United States. The booking calendar is not loaded until you ask for it, either with the button on the booking page or through the booking button at the top of each page.

Once it is loaded, your browser connects to Calendly and to services Calendly’s page uses, which set cookies and store items on your device. Calendly shows its own cookie banner inside the calendar, where you can accept or decline its optional cookies, and its privacy notice (external site) explains how it uses cookies.

Changing your choice

Your choice is stored in your browser. With JavaScript turned on, this page shows it here and lets you clear it.

Clearing your choice means we will ask again before loading Calendly. It does not delete what Calendly and the services it uses have already stored: you can delete that, or block it, in your browser’s settings for those sites, such as calendly.com.

Need help now?

DeepRest is not an emergency service and cannot respond to urgent requests. If you need help now, please don’t wait for a reply from us.

  • 999

    Emergency. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, or a life is at risk, call 999 or go to your nearest A&E.

  • NHS 111

    Urgent, but not an emergency. Call 111 for urgent medical help or advice.

  • 116 123

    Samaritans. Free to call, day or night, 365 days a year, whatever you’re going through.
